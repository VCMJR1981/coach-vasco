The plateau isn't a fitness problem

You paddle out consistently. You catch waves. And you still surf the same way you did two seasons ago. That's not a lack of ability, and it's rarely a lack of effort.

It's usually one of three things: you're training the wrong variable, you're carrying cue overload into every wave, or fear is quietly changing your timing before it changes your movement. None of those get fixed by more sessions.

  • You surf fine on small days and freeze when it gets bigger.
  • Your bottom turn works — sometimes. You can't say why.
  • You surfskate a lot and nothing transfers to the water.
  • You've had lessons. You got tips. You didn't get a plan.
  • You're strong in the gym and still gassed after 40 minutes.

CSTM

The Concrete Surfers Training Method

A step-by-step system for surfskate as a sport in its own right — and as the most honest surf training tool there is, when it's used properly.

Safety first, easy to follow, built for the fastest realistic path to skill. It teaches timing, not movements. If a student is thinking, they're already late.

“Smooth surfskating doesn't mean effective surfskating.”

Level 1 — the foundations

  1. Stance & balance — feet placement, knee flexion, centre of gravity, upper body alignment
  2. Pumping & speed generation — weight transfer, compression and extension, upper body sync
  3. Bottom turn frontside — compression setup, back arm, timing, head position
  4. Bottom turn backside — front arm lead, compression, timely extension
  5. Carving frontside — low centred stance, shoulders lead, look where you go
  6. Carving backside — front arm as pivot, shoulder rotation, eyes first

About

I'm Vasco. I coach surfers who are done guessing.

Ten-plus years coaching surf and surfskate, most of it with people who already surf and can't work out why they've stopped getting better. I built Concrete Surfers because European surfskate didn't need another brand — it needed clearer standards.

My bias is simple: coaching that doesn't change how you read a wave is incomplete. A good coach reduces information. So expect fewer cues from me, and better ones.

I work with beginners and intermediates. If you're chasing high performance, there are specialists better suited to you and I'll say so.

Questions

Do I have to buy the diagnostic first?

It's strongly recommended. Without it I'm coaching blind and you're paying for guesses. If you've worked with me before, book the session directly.

What footage do you need?

For surf: 3–5 waves, filmed from the beach or a phone gimbal, any angle. For surfskate: one clip on flat ground and one on a ramp or bank. Phone quality is fine.

I surfskate but I don't surf. Is this for me?

Yes. Surfskate is a sport in its own right and I coach it as one. The video analysis session works the same way.

What's the difference between the two 80€ sessions?

Video analysis works on what you do on the board. Strength & conditioning works on what your body can hold while you do it. The diagnostic tells you which one you need first.

Which languages do you coach in?

English and Portuguese.

How do I pay, and what if I need to reschedule?

You'll get a payment link by email once we've confirmed a slot. Reschedule for free with 24 hours' notice.
